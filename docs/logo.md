# Logo

Original generated artwork for this project. A pearl-glass ribbon with a continuous soft fold on a cobalt tile. The mark is independent of the product name and works as a macOS icon.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. `Resources/Logo.png` is the original PNG; `scripts/make-icon.sh` produces the macOS ICNS sizes.

Prompt: “Create one polished original macOS app icon for a small utility that makes the desktop flow and softly blur as a laptop lid folds. Single centered abstract ribbon of frosted pearl glass bent in a graceful soft S / shallow hinge, two broad planes with a continuous rounded fold, communicating flexibility and fluid motion. Restrained premium native Mac aesthetic, crisp simple silhouette legible at 32 pixels, subtle cool blue refraction at the fold. Deep saturated cobalt-blue rounded square tile with standard macOS icon proportions and generous 10% outer transparent padding. No letters, no wordmark, no devices, no Apple logo, no extra symbols, no mockup, no surrounding scene.”
