Source: [João Franco, X](https://x.com/JoaoFranco_03/status/2098069223171916209). The inspected stream is 1920×1080 at 30 fps: 469 frames, 15.633 seconds. All frames 000–468 were decoded and visually inspected in sequence, in thirty indexed sheets of sixteen frames (the last sheet has five). No frame selection, scene-cut skipping or temporal subsampling was used in this pass. Native-size frames at 3.0, 4.5 and 6.0 seconds supplement the sheets. Reference images/video remain private research material, not repository assets.

## Geometric distinction

Between the native-size 4.5-second and 6-second frames, the physical top edge moves downward by roughly 140 pixels while the central food image remains at nearly the same height. Camera motion and hand occlusion make these approximate screen-space observations, not calibrated physical measurements. The reference is consistent with perspective compensation that holds the image in the viewer's space and allows the physical panel boundary to cross it. Pinning pixels to the panel or stretching the entire page to fill the visible region produces a different illusion.

## Limits

The clip has no hinge telemetry or viewer calibration, and it shows one full closing/opening cycle. It does not establish an exact angle threshold, sub-frame input latency, private shader coefficients or general behavior during long pauses. The candidate's small-step onset and stuck-overlay protections come from the user's requested behavior. Do not describe those numeric settings as extracted from the clip.
